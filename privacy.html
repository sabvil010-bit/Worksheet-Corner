The small print, kept small

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

The short version: Worksheet Corner is free, has no accounts and no ads, and doesn't collect information from students. You never need to sign in to print a worksheet.

Who we are

Worksheet Corner publishes free printable worksheets for students aged 5–14 at worksheetcorner.site. If you have a question about this policy, email hello@worksheetcorner.site.

Students

  • Students can browse and print every worksheet without creating an account or typing in any personal details.
  • There are no ads, chat, comments, or places to upload photos.
  • Our request form and newsletter are meant for teachers, parents and carers. If you're a student, please ask an adult to contact us for you. If we learn that a child under 13 has sent us personal information, we'll delete it.

What we collect from adults

  • Things you send us. If you join the Sunday list, we keep your email address. If you request a worksheet, we keep what you write in the form, your email address and, if you add it, your first name.
  • Basic server logs. Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard request information (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to keep the site running and secure. These logs are kept for a short period and are not used to build profiles.

How we use it

  • To send the Sunday list email, if you signed up for it.
  • To reply to your worksheet request or question.
  • To decide which worksheets to write next, using request topics in aggregate.

We never sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Cookies and third parties

We don't use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or social media pixels. Our fonts are served by Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to load them, and Google may receive your IP address as part of that request.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your email for as long as you're on the Sunday list, and request details for as long as they're useful for planning new sheets. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete anything we hold about you by emailing hello@worksheetcorner.site.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll mention it in the Sunday list.
