Fair use of our sheets

Terms of use

Last updated: 14 September 2026

The short version: Worksheet Corner is free. Print and copy our worksheets as much as you like for your students or your own children. Please don't resell them or re-upload the files.

Using the worksheets

Worksheets on worksheetcorner.site are free for classroom, school, homeschool, tutoring and personal use. There are no accounts, subscriptions or paid tiers. You're welcome to:

  • Print or photocopy as many copies as your students or children need.
  • Show a worksheet on a classroom screen or document camera.
  • Share a link to our site or to a specific worksheet with students, families and colleagues.
  • Use the sheets in a paid tutoring session, as long as you aren't charging for the sheet itself.

What we ask you not to do

  • Sell the worksheets, or include them in a product that is sold.
  • Re-upload the files to another website, marketplace or file-sharing service.
  • Remove the Worksheet Corner name from the pages.

A student-safe site

We keep Worksheet Corner free of ads, pop-ups, chat and user comments so it's suitable for use in schools. We don't link to shops or ask students for information. If you ever find something on the site that doesn't feel right for students, please tell us and we'll look at it straight away.

Ownership

The worksheets, illustrations and text on this site belong to Worksheet Corner. Using them under these terms doesn't transfer ownership.

Accuracy

Every sheet is written and checked with care, but mistakes can slip through. If you spot one, please tell us at hello@worksheetcorner.site and we'll fix it. The site and worksheets are provided as they are, without guarantees.

Supervision

Some activities involve scissors, glue or crayons — younger students should use them with an adult nearby.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when they last changed.

Contact

Questions about these terms? Email hello@worksheetcorner.site.
